Our Story

Bywater started with a simple idea: what if we could bring the centuries-old tradition of sauna and cold plunge to Seattle's incredible waterfront? We wanted to create a space that honors the ancient practice while making it accessible and welcoming to everyone.

We chose the name "Bywater" to reflect our commitment to bringing wellness experiences directly to the water's edge. Each of our four locations offers something unique - from the sunset views at Golden Gardens to the skyline panoramas at Alki Beach.

What started as a passion project has grown into a thriving community of people who believe in the power of hot and cold therapy. We're honored to be part of your wellness journey and excited to continue growing alongside the Seattle community.

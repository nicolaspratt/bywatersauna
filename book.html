Book a Session

Ready to experience heat and cold therapy? Reserve your spot online - it's as easy as signing up for a class.

How It Works

Booking a Bywater session is simple:

  • Choose your preferred location and time slot
  • Select session length (60 or 75 minutes)
  • Pay securely online ($35 weekday, $40 weekend)
  • Receive confirmation email with details
  • Show up and enjoy your session!

What to Bring

Just bring yourself, a towel, and a swimsuit! We provide everything else you need including outdoor showers, towel storage, and a welcoming community.

Cancellation Policy

Cancel anytime up to 24 hours before your session for a full credit. Late cancellations and no-shows cannot be refunded, so we can open your spot to someone on the waitlist.

First Time?

New to sauna and cold plunge? Don't worry! We recommend starting with a 60-minute session and cycling between hot and cold at your own pace. There's no wrong way to do it - listen to your body and enjoy the journey. Our friendly community is always happy to share tips!

Steps to purchase

  1. Log into your account on our booking site and click 'Profile' in the top right corner
  2. Click 'My membership' and then 'Buy a membership'
  3. Select the 'Bywater Membership' option

Membership Terms

Guest Passes

We love friends, and want to meet yours! Members get one free guest pass per month. To use your guest pass, just add +1 to your booking.

For additional guests beyond the free pass, the member's account will be charged for the session at the drop-in rate. Our app will allow you to book for more than one guest per month without any additional fee at the time of booking.

Booking Caps

As a member, you can attend unlimited sessions per month, with a few caveats:

  • One session per day
  • Two upcoming bookings at any given time (ie. If you have bookings for 12/1 and 12/4, you will not be able to book another session until after your 12/1 visit)

Cancellation / No-Shows

Cancel anytime up to 12 hours in advance of your session.

For no-shows and late cancellations, there is a $20 penalty for the members. If the booking includes guests beyond a guest pass, there is an additional $35 per guest.

Emergencies

We do not want to be punitive. We do want to encourage responsibility, communication, and grace.

If you have an emergency, please reach out to contact us and let us know.

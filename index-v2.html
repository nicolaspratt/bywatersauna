QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your Bywater sauna experience. Can't find what you're looking for?

Is that a sauna? How does this all work? ▼
Yes, it is a sauna! We offer sauna sessions at the beach. Sessions last either 60 or 75 minutes. Anyone can book a spot during a session - reserve online beforehand like signing up for a workout class. During your session, cycle between the sauna and cold water at your own pace.
Are there rules or specific etiquette? ▼
Yes! Wash sandy toes before entering. Sit on a towel inside the sauna (required). Take your towel with you when you go for a dip - no saving seats. Mind the door and close it quickly. Let folks know before pouring water on rocks. Respect your fellow saunamates - Bywater is a shared space.
How should I start and end my session? ▼
You're the boss of your cycling schedule! Start and end on cold or hot - whatever feels right. Take a break after 15-20 consecutive minutes in the sauna to allow your core temperature to come down. Any amount of cold water exposure complements sauna use.
Where do I put my stuff? ▼
We have shelves and hooks available outside the sauna for guests' things. We recommend leaving all non-essential items at home. Leave heat-sensitive items outside: jewelry, eyeglasses, phones, cameras, watches, etc.
Are there showers available? ▼
Yes, but not private showers. The bathhouses have quick rinse outdoor showers available to rinse off before and after your session.
Are people under 18 allowed? ▼
With prior notice and when accompanied by a parent or guardian, yes! Please reach out directly to info@bywatersauna.com if one of your guests is under 18.
How does the wait list work? ▼
Add your name to the waitlist when a session is full. When a spot opens up, everyone on the waitlist receives an email notification. Availability is first come, first served. The waitlist cannot accommodate multi-spot bookings - each person must sign up individually.
What is your cancellation policy? ▼
Cancel anytime up to 24 hours before the start of a session to receive a credit back to your Bywater account. Bookings are non-refundable. Late cancellations (less than 24 hours) and no-shows do not receive credit.

Who We Are

Bywater brings the ancient ritual of sauna to Seattle's most beautiful waterfront locations. We're building a third space where strangers become neighbors, and wellness meets authentic community.

Bywater is not meant to be luxury, it's meant to be social. We offer outdoor sauna sessions at the beach where you can cycle between heat and cold at your own pace. Book a spot, show up with a towel, and experience the simple joy of shared heat and cold plunges with your community.

This is wellness without the pretense. This is community by water.

How It Works

Sessions last 60-75 minutes. You control your own experience, cycling between heat and cold at your own pace.

1

Book a Session

Reserve your spot online. Choose your location and time. Limited spots per session—book like a workout class.

2

Heat Up 🔥

Take a seat in our saunas. Step out anytime, but take a break after 15-20 consecutive minutes.

3

Cool Down ❄️

Dip into the cold water (or enjoy the cool air). Any amount of cold exposure complements sauna use.

4

Repeat 🔄

Cycle between hot and cold at your own pace. Start and end on hot or cold—you're the boss.

Good to Know

  • ✓ Bring a towel (required to sit on in the sauna)
  • ✓ Leave valuables at home—shelves available for essentials
  • ✓ Rinse off sandy feet before entering
  • ✓ Close the door quickly to keep the heat in
  • ✓ Let others know before pouring water on rocks
  • ✓ Respect your fellow saunamates—Bywater is a shared space

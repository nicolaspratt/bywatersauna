Who We Are

Bywater Sauna is Seattle's communal wellness space designed for connection, not excess. We're building a third space where the city's heartbeat slows down, conversations flow freely, and the ancient ritual of sauna brings strangers together.

Forget spa luxury and performative wellness. We're about authentic connection, shared heat, and the kind of real conversations that happen when you sit still long enough to meet your neighbors.

This is community by water.
